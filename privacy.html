Privacy Policy

NFP Publisher. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

What this app is

NFP Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Odyssey Beyond to manage that channel.

What data it accesses

With your permission, through Google OAuth, it accesses your YouTube account data: channel and video information, statistics, and the ability to upload or update your own videos, captions and thumbnails.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the actions you request on your own channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose. Requests are sent through Composio, a service that securely stores the access token and calls the YouTube API on your behalf.

Storage and deletion

No YouTube data is kept beyond what is needed to perform a request. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

NFP Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
